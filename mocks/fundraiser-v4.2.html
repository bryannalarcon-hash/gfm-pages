SJ Sarah J. · May 12, 2026

When wildfire moves, minutes decide everything. Watch Duty’s volunteer network of reporters, pilots, and dispatchers tracks active fires and pushes real-time evacuation alerts to the people in their path — free, ad-free, and built for the moment the sky turns orange.

This campaign funds the alerting infrastructure: the servers, the on-call reporting desk, and the mapping that turns scattered scanner traffic into a single trustworthy notification on your phone. Every dollar extends coverage to another county before the next fire season.
