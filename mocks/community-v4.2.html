About Watch Duty

Watch Duty is a nonprofit dedicated to keeping communities safe from wildfire. A network of volunteer reporters, off-duty firefighters, and dispatchers track active fires and share real-time information so people can make informed decisions during an emergency.

Community guidelines

Be kind, stay on-topic, and never share information that could compromise an active response. Posts are moderated by community organizers.
